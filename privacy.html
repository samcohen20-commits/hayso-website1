2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the personal information we collect through our websites at haysohealth.com, hayso.co.uk and haysohealth.co.uk (the "Site"), and through any emails we send you about the launch.

It does not cover the hayso app or clinic dashboard, which are not yet available. Before we onboard any clinic we will publish a separate, more detailed privacy notice, and we will put a written data processing agreement in place with each clinic covering patient information.

3. What we collect

Information you give us on the waiting-list form

  • Your full name.
  • Your work email address.
  • Your phone number, if you choose to give it.
  • The name of your clinic or workplace.
  • Your role, if you choose to give it.
  • The approximate number of patients your clinic sees who are on GLP-1 treatment.
  • A record of your consent: the date and time you submitted the form, the page you submitted it from, and the exact wording you agreed to.

Most of this is business information rather than information about your private life. Because it is attached to you by name, we treat all of it as personal information and handle it accordingly.

Information we collect automatically

  • Referral and campaign information — where you arrived from, including any UTM parameters in the link you clicked (for example utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign).
  • Technical information — your IP address, browser type and device type, and the date and time of your visit. This is generated by our hosting provider as part of delivering and securing the Site.
  • Analytics information, if you consent to analytics cookies — which pages you viewed and how you moved through the Site, together with a randomly generated identifier that lets us recognise a return visit. We do not use it to identify you personally and it is not linked to your waiting-list record. See our Cookie Policy for detail.

What we deliberately do not collect

We do not collect any information about patients. The patient number on our form is an approximate count for your clinic as a whole — we do not want, and you should not send us, names, records, or any other information about the people your clinic treats.

We do not collect information about your own health, and you should not send us any. If you send us health information anyway, we will delete it.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

6. Sending information outside the UK

Your waiting-list record and any analytics information are stored within the European Economic Area — in Ireland and Germany respectively. The UK government has determined that EEA countries provide an adequate level of data protection, so information can move from the UK to them without additional safeguards.

Two things do involve information leaving the UK and the EEA:

  • Site hosting. Our hosting provider operates a global network and may serve pages, and hold short-term server logs, outside the UK and EEA — including in the United States.
  • Provider support access. Supabase and PostHog are US-incorporated companies. Although they store our data in the EEA, their staff may access it from the United States for support and maintenance.

Where that happens we rely on the safeguards permitted by UK data protection law — the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses together with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, plus the provider's certification under the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where applicable.

You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards by emailing privacy@haysohealth.com.

7. How long we keep it

  • Waiting-list submissions: until hayso launches and for up to 12 months after, or until you ask us to delete your record — whichever comes first. If you become a customer, a separate retention period will apply and we will tell you what it is.
  • If you unsubscribe: we keep a minimal suppression record so we don't accidentally contact you again. This is kept until you ask us to remove it entirely.
  • Consent records: for as long as we hold your record, plus 12 months.
  • Server logs: 30 days.

If hayso does not launch, we will delete the waiting list within 3 months of deciding not to proceed.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how we use your information — that's what this policy is for.
  • Access a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Rectification — have inaccurate information corrected.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your information.
  • Restriction — ask us to pause using your information while a dispute is resolved.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Data portability — receive your information in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time. This won't affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@haysohealth.com. We will respond within one month.

To stop hearing from us, use the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or email us. This takes effect immediately.

There is no charge for exercising your rights.

10. Security

Waiting-list submissions are held in a database with row-level security enabled, configured so that the Site can add a submission but cannot read the table back. All connections are encrypted and the Site is served over HTTPS. Access to the stored data is limited to Samuel Seymour Lionel Cohen and [ANY DEVELOPER WITH ACCESS].

No system is completely secure, but if a breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours and tell you where we are required to.
