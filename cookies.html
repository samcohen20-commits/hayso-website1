Hayso — Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies — local storage, pixels, tracking scripts — work in comparable ways. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

Under the UK's Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), we must ask your permission before placing any cookie that isn't strictly necessary for the Site to work.

2. Cookies we use

Strictly necessary — no consent required

These are needed for the Site to function or to remember your privacy choices. They are set regardless of your preferences.

Marketing and advertising

We do not currently use any advertising or marketing cookies. We don't run ad pixels, and we don't share information about your visit with advertising networks.

What isn't a cookie

The campaign information in the links we share (UTM parameters such as utm_source and utm_campaign) travels in the web address itself, not in a cookie. We store it alongside your waiting-list sign-up so we know which channel brought you to us. This is explained in our Privacy Policy.

3. Managing your choices

On this Site: click Cookie settings in the footer at any time to change or withdraw your consent. Rejecting non-essential cookies won't stop you from using the Site or joining the waiting list.

In your browser: you can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, parts of the Site may not work properly. Instructions are available from your browser's help pages.

4. Changes

We'll update this policy when we add or remove cookies. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
